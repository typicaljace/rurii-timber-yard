About Us

Welcome to our timber yard and furniture store — where craftsmanship meets quality. We specialize in premium timber, custom furniture, and reliable woodworking services designed to meet the needs of builders, DIYers, and home improvement lovers alike.

With years of hands-on experience, we take pride in providing expertly cut timber, unique handcrafted pieces, and a range of services including timber splitting, sawdust supply, and furniture refinishing.

Whether you're looking for raw materials or ready-to-use furniture, our team is here to help you bring your vision to life.
